## Common questions

**Why does sharing from Rightmove or Zoopla only save a link?**
Those sites do not allow apps to read their pages, and their apps share only the link. Use the address search on the form, then type the price and bedrooms. OnTheMarket listings fill in more. The Help page in Settings explains this.

**My properties are not appearing on my other device.**
Both devices must be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and **Sync with iCloud** must be on in the app's Settings. Give it a minute, and open the app on both.

**Can I stop it using my iCloud storage?**
Yes: turn off **Sync with iCloud** in Settings and reopen the app. Your data then stays on that device only, so export a copy now and then.

**How do I get my data out?**
Settings > Export Properties as CSV, or the menu on a property > Share Summary (PDF).

**How do I delete everything?**
Settings > Delete All Data. With sync on, this also removes it from iCloud and your other devices.
