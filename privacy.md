# Homebuyer Shortlist: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 3 November 2026*

**Homebuyer Shortlist** is a personal notebook for people buying a home. This page explains what happens to the information you put into it. The short version: it stays with you.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter: properties, addresses, prices, notes, criteria and scores, contacts you add, visit notes and photos you take or choose, and web links.

## Where it is kept

- **On your device.** All of it is stored on your iPhone or iPad.
- **In your own iCloud account, if sync is on.** Sync is on by default and can be turned off in Settings. When it is on, Apple's iCloud keeps your data in your private iCloud storage so your devices stay in step. It counts towards your iCloud storage. The developer cannot see it.

The developer has no servers, no accounts and no database of users. **No information from the app is sent to, or collected by, the developer.**

## What the app does not do

- It does not track you, and it contains no advertising or analytics.
- It does not sell or share your data.
- It does not ask for your location. Address suggestions use what you type, not where you are.

## Things that connect to the internet

- **Address search.** When you type an address, the text is sent to Apple Maps to find matching places, under Apple's privacy policy.
- **OnTheMarket links.** If you share an OnTheMarket listing into the app, the app asks that site once for the page's title and description so it can fill in the form. OnTheMarket can see this request as it would see any visit. No other property site is contacted by the app.
- **Web links.** Links you save open in the in-app browser (Safari), which loads the site you chose.
- **iCloud.** As above, if you leave sync on.

## Permissions

- **Camera**, to photograph properties you visit.
- **Photos (add only)**, only if you turn on "Keep camera photos in my Photos library" in Settings. The app never reads your photo library. Choosing existing photos uses Apple's own picker, which shares only the photos you pick.
- **Contacts.** The app has no access to your contacts. When you choose "Fill from Contacts App", Apple's picker passes the app only the one contact you select.

## Your choices and deleting your data

- Turn iCloud sync off in Settings.
- Export your properties as a CSV file, or one property as a PDF, from Settings or the property's menu. You decide where the file goes.
- Delete everything with **Settings > Delete All Data**. With sync on, this also removes it from iCloud and your other devices. Deleting the app removes the data held on that device; manage what is stored in iCloud under Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

## Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
